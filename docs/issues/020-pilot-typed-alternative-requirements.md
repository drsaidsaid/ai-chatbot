# Pilot typed alternative requirements

Status: Integrated; local pilot acceptance passed, not deployed

The pilot needs typed `all`/`any` Offer requirement groups for alternatives
such as no business *or* an operating business below a configured revenue
threshold. Groups remain bounded typed comparisons, are conjunctive with
existing requirements, and never activate a sales call without explicit Lead
agreement. See ADR 0017 and the concise amendments in `CONTEXT.md`,
`TECHNICAL_DESIGN.md`, and the product agreement.

Acceptance includes branch-aware missing questions, revision-driven stale
authority, readable owner preview, and preservation of existing flat-rule
behavior.
