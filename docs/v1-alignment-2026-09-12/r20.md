## What to build

A Business Account admin publishes a price once and a Lead receives only the applicable approved commercial facts.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create/edit/publish Offer amount and currency, quote-required mode, effective dates/timezone, conditions, promotional price and optional approved pricing/purchase link in a plain-language Offer view.
- [ ] Document extraction creates proposals for approval; contradictory document prices flag review and cannot silently replace the authoritative Offer price. Lead budget and platform plan price remain separate.
- [ ] Pricing response uses current selected Offer, published revision and applicable conditions. Missing, expired, future, conflicting or uncertain eligibility never produces a guessed quote or link.
- [ ] Preview and inbound reply use the same resolution path; edits invalidate affected pending quotations at final send without changing historic evidence.
- [ ] Demonstrate standard/promotion boundaries, timezone transitions, conflicting sources, quote-required mode, stale queued output, multi-account isolation and readable desktop/phone UI. No unconfirmed OPU price is seeded live.
