## What to build

A business selling consultations can propose its paid Offer and book only after its team verifies the Lead payment.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Configure optional consultation Offer trigger/conditions, approved price, duration, payment instructions and calendar. Other businesses remain unaffected and can use free/direct-purchase paths.
- [ ] For an applicable strategy request disclose approved price, obtain interest, send approved payment instructions and create an awaiting-payment record linked to Lead/Offer/revision. Do not promise an unreserved slot.
- [ ] An authorised Business Account team member confirms payment using amount/currency/reference and audit; a Lead statement or screenshot alone cannot authorise booking. This is separate from platform Subscription payment authority.
- [ ] After confirmation offer current available times and book the Lead-agreed slot through R13. Enforce one entitlement/booking per payment purpose; duplicate confirmations/events do not duplicate appointments.
- [ ] If no slot, provider failure, currency mismatch or policy uncertainty occurs, preserve payment record and create reviewable human work; never imply a refund or completed Booking without evidence.
- [ ] Test separate account/platform roles, unpaid blocking, partial/wrong payments, replay, double-booking race, appointment changes and non-consultation businesses. Demonstrate test payment confirmation and calendar fixture, then authorised calendar proof.
