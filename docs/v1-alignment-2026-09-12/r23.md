## What to build

A client pays manually, the Platform Operator activates a monthly plan once, and the client can see an enforceable monthly usage allowance.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Provide platform-managed draft/published plans with currency, monthly price and allowance; client sees selected plan, renewal date, used/remaining units and percentage. Do not publish illustrative tariffs as agreed prices.
- [ ] Client requests subscription and sees approved payment instructions; an authorised Platform Operator confirms amount/currency/reference and activates once. Duplicate submissions cannot duplicate allowances; Business Account admins cannot self-confirm platform payments.
- [ ] Customer usage ledger reserves before AI work and settles at most one logical reply at confirmed canonical send. Bubbles, internal calls, retries and duplicate callbacks do not multiply charges. Failed/uncertain/partial responses have explicit conservative reconciliation without unsafe resend.
- [ ] Monthly allowance resets at renewal boundary, with deterministic timezone/calendar behavior, concurrent request protection and no artificial reset from model changes. At exhaustion pause AI, preserve inbound capture/human inbox, show top-up/upgrade action and alert the owner.
- [ ] Show AI subscription/credits separately from Meta messaging billed directly and advertising spend. Track actual provider costs and owner revenue/cost completeness separately; templates with no AI work are not charged as AI replies.
- [ ] Tests cover replay, rollback, crashes, competing messages, period rollover, insufficient entitlement, provider failure, multi-bubble partial send, authorisation, stale UI and phone usage meter. Existing provider reserve/canonical delivery controls remain intact.
