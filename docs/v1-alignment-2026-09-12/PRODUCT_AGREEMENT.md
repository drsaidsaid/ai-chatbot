The owner approved the grilling recommendations and explicitly requested tickets and resumed delivery. This agreement supersedes conflicting earlier V1 product assumptions; it is not proof that the new behavior is implemented. AI Lead Employee remains the working name. ## The promise

Help businesses with high WhatsApp enquiry volume, especially click-to-WhatsApp ads, answer buying questions, identify suitable and ready leads, and progress them toward the business's chosen next step. Do not promise that qualified leads will buy. Build in the owned Community Edition Rails/Vue product; preserve tenancy, MIT notice, human control, consent, durable delivery and evidence. No enterprise source or parallel frontend.

## Simple setup and optional qualification

One guided Business setup presents About the business, Products and services, Prices and promotions, Who is a good fit, and What happens next. Assisted setup is the first operating model. Owners can write or import documents; the app extracts proposed facts and rules into a plain-language preview. Only an authorised owner publication activates them. Preserve provenance, versions, correction history and current runtime/test parity.

An Offer can be a product, service, programme, consultation or other business proposition. Qualification has explicit not-configured, disabled and enabled states. Missing criteria never restore a fixed interview or imply rejection. Approved answers can operate while incomplete action prerequisites remain blocked. Owners deliberately choose answer-only, purchase/enquiry link, qualification before a sales call, or appointment flow. No business must offer a call or a paid consultation.

Questions follow approved information requirements, not a fixed script. Ask at most one useful question when appropriate, skip known facts, clarify uncertain facts and accept corrections. Separate business fit, readiness to speak/buy and action eligibility. Required evidence and strictness are business-defined; no universal revenue, budget, urgency or authority rules. Operators can flag poor-fit/not-ready handoffs and propose reviewed configuration changes; no silent self-modification.

## Knowledge and commercial facts

Answer relevant buying questions using approved scoped information. Avoid free personalised consulting: acknowledge a strategy question and ask a useful fit question, offer an approved resource, or propose an applicable paid service. No arbitrary two-answer quota. Unrelated requests get a polite boundary. A relevant unknown answer receives a truthful acknowledgment and actual Review Request; do not promise a callback or resolution time that has not been arranged.

Each Offer has one authoritative published price/promotion entry with currency, amount or explicit quote-required state, conditions, effective dates/timezone and optional approved pricing/purchase link. Document import can propose entries; conflicting prose cannot override published prices. Customer budget is not an Offer price. No inactive, future, expired or inapplicable promotion may be quoted. Missing current prices block quotations, not all conversation.

## Human help and bookings

Sales-call eligibility follows current approved fit/readiness requirements and the Lead's agreement. Basic human assistance remains available without promising an owner appointment. A person declining the bot must not be trapped in repeated qualification. Human takeover pauses AI until explicit resume. Human help, Review Request and sales-call queues have clear purposes and routing.

Google Calendar remains the first calendar provider. Paid consultation is optional Offer configuration. Suggest it only under approved business conditions, disclose its price, obtain interest, provide approved payment instructions, and wait for an authorised team member's verified payment record before offering/booking the Lead's chosen available slot. A screenshot or 'I paid' message is not proof. Preserve payment-to-offer and booking linkage; prevent duplicate confirmation/booking. Cancellation/refund decisions go to a human unless an approved policy explicitly provides the permitted behavior.

## Ads, templates and follow-up

Owners can map an entire ad set to an Offer, including current and future ads, with optional individual-ad overrides. Specific ad overrides take precedence over ad-set rules; an explicit conversation Offer selection is not silently replaced by later metadata. Resolve provider source data through permitted Meta access, retain attribution/provenance, and use clarification/default behavior when absent or unresolved. Never guess eligibility from campaign membership. This is inbound routing, not an ad-management product.

Templates and bounded broadcasts are now in V1 scope. Owners draft, preview and submit templates to Meta, see approval/rejection/paused status, and select consent-eligible recipients for send/schedule with reviewable estimated Meta charges and delivery results. Static template fan-out does not consume one AI reply credit per recipient. No broad autonomous outbound marketing, unknown cold lists or WhatsApp Status/story publishing is approved. 'Status messages' is interpreted only as direct business updates until clarified.

Default unfinished-conversation follow-up is at most one contextual reminder plus one friendly closing invitation; timing is configurable. Stop sales chasing on decline, opt-out, payment, booking, closure or human ownership. Necessary transaction/booking messages are separate actions with their own authority. Hesitation is not refusal. Recheck current consent, control, source/promotion validity and WhatsApp permission before send. Do not assume a universal 72-hour free-form window; ordinary 24-hour user-message permission and special free-entry pricing windows are distinct. Use an approved eligible template outside the applicable free-form window or suppress the send. No unlimited retries.

## Managed AI, subscriptions and costs

Clients pay the platform owner for AI and software, and pay Meta separately for billable WhatsApp usage under direct onboarding. Advertising spend is also separate. Clearly distinguish AI credits, Meta messaging and ad spend before purchase and in Billing. Provider secrets and model selection belong to platform operations, not Business Account admins. Enforce this server-side and in the UI; preserve per-account metering and tenant isolation during migration.

Start with manually verified subscription payments, later substituting online verified payment events through the same idempotent entitlement boundary. One customer-billable unit is a completed logical AI reply successfully confirmed sent by the canonical provider delivery path; splitting bubbles does not multiply it. Internal model calls, retries, failed outputs, duplicate delivery and static template broadcasts do not multiply customer charges. Track provider costs separately even when not customer-billable. Reserve allowance before AI work; keep uncertain outcomes conservative and reconcile without unsafe resend. Billing tests must cover concurrency, partial multi-message output and crashes so neither duplicate charges nor free runaway work arise.

Customers see usage percentage, used/remaining units, renewal date and top-up/upgrade actions. Included usage resets at the subscription renewal date. Purchased extras carry forward while the subscription is active; use monthly allowance first. No retroactive deletion of purchased balances. At exhaustion pause AI, preserve inbound capture/human inbox, alert the owner and require paid top-up/upgrade. No automatic charges or silent downgrade. Default billing is monthly; exact package amounts/allowances are unpublished hypotheses until cost evaluation.

Manual payment confirmation activates the purchased entitlement once, with actor/reference/amount/currency/audit. A mid-cycle upgrade charges the current cycle's plan-price difference, replaces total included allowance with the higher plan allowance, preserves usage and top-ups, and keeps the renewal date. Example: 1000 to 3000 after 800 used leaves 2200 included units. Top-ups cost more per unit than larger-plan included usage; do not advertise a fixed 20% saving without validated configured prices. Cancellation, refunds, downgrades and disputed payments are manual review in V1, not inferred automatic actions.

The owner sees revenue, provider charges, Meta reporting separately, hosting/payment/support allocations and estimated contribution margin per Business Account. Missing provider cost remains unknown/pending, never zero. Compare candidate models on fixed English/Swahili conversations, qualified/non-qualified/no-qualification paths, grounded prices, corrections, unknown questions and human/consent controls. Measure total cost, speed and response quality; reject models failing critical cases regardless of price. One tested quality standard for every plan. Benchmark uses authorised non-customer fixtures and an explicit spend cap before paid runs; no unlimited spending permission is implied.
