## What to build

An authorised Business Account admin drafts a template, submits it to Meta and sees whether it can be sent.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Add Templates under Settings → WhatsApp using existing CE layout. Draft text/media/buttons/variables/language/category and provide a recipient-style preview and validation.
- [ ] Submit through account-authorised Meta APIs; retain immutable submitted content/revision and provider ID, approval, rejection reason, paused/disabled status and last-sync time. Retry uncertain submission through reconciliation, not duplicate creation.
- [ ] Only approved currently sendable provider revisions may be selected; edits/resubmissions do not mislabel an old version as newly approved. Never claim Meta approval from local validation.
- [ ] Display estimated Meta charges with category/market/date/source and clearly identify direct Meta billing, separate from AI allowance. Missing pricing cannot be represented as zero.
- [ ] Demonstrate tenant/role isolation, provider error/rejection/late status changes, phone preview and a real authorised template approval path. Drafting/submission does not authorise sending customer messages.
- [ ] No WhatsApp Status/story publishing. Template authoring is manual in this slice; AI-assisted writing and its billing are not implied.
