## What to build

The Platform Operator selects the tested AI service while a Business Account admin sees service readiness and usage without managing provider keys.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Implement a platform-authorised provider/model control path, distinct from Business Account admin; clients cannot read, modify, health-check or infer provider credentials through UI or APIs.
- [ ] Migrate account-owned R10 connections with an explicit reversible transition plan; retain encrypted storage, redaction, current account attribution, provider health, privacy settings and revision-sensitive delivery authority.
- [ ] All client UI routes remove provider/model/key controls and instead explain managed AI service and account-specific readiness. Server policy protects direct and stale API access, not only hidden navigation.
- [ ] Preserve per-account usage/cost isolation even with platform-managed credentials; one account cannot consume or inspect another account entitlement. Cost absent remains unknown.
- [ ] Run account-admin/member/platform-operator tests, in-flight configuration-change checks, credential-leak checks and desktop/phone acceptance. Do not rotate live credentials or enable provider spending without concrete authorised release preparation.
