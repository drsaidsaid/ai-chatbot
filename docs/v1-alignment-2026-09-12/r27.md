## What to build

A Business Account admin previews a permitted audience and sends or schedules an approved template broadcast with honest delivery results.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Add a focused Broadcasts destination under More using CE conventions, with list/create/detail and Templates shortcut; avoid a second template editor or a general marketing workflow builder.
- [ ] Snapshot selected eligible recipients and template revision, deduplicate recipients, exclude missing/withdrawn promotional consent, and show exclusions, variables, schedule, estimated Meta charges and explicit final send action.
- [ ] Recheck consent, human control, template availability, recipient eligibility and permitted channel rules at each send. Scheduling or preview does not bypass later opt-out/human takeover. Respect provider limits and bound jobs.
- [ ] Use canonical durable outbound delivery and existing uncertain-outcome rules; never resend accepted/unknown work blindly. Cancellation prevents unsent items without claiming recall of already sent items.
- [ ] Static template fan-out consumes no AI reply units merely per recipient; AI responses to resulting Lead messages follow ordinary metering. Record broadcast/Offer provenance without silently changing an already explicit Conversation Offer.
- [ ] Show queued/sent/delivered/read/failed/unknown separately, reconcile duplicates and explain final costs versus estimates. Test replays/concurrency/control changes/timezone/scheduling/invalid variables, followed by authorised test-recipient-only proof. No real audience send is pre-authorised by implementation.
