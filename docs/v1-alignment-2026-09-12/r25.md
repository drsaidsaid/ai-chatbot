## What to build

A Business Account admin links an ad set once and Leads from its current and newly added ads receive the intended Offer context.

Implement one complete owned Rails/Vue path across storage, permissions, API, jobs/provider and UI. Follow the approved 12 September product agreement and ADR 0016. Preserve MIT, tenant isolation, consent, canonical delivery and current evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Connect authorised Meta ad metadata and configure ad-set-to-Offer mapping with optional individual-ad override. Prove required access/permissions and store account-owned provider IDs without exposing tokens.
- [ ] All current/future member ads inherit the ad-set mapping without per-ad setup; resolve/cache/sync membership with defined freshness and disabled/deleted/revoked access behavior.
- [ ] Specific ad override wins over ad-set rule. Record source, mapping revision and selection reason. Existing explicit Conversation Offer selection is never silently overwritten by a later referral; changing Offer is an explicit recorded action.
- [ ] Missing/unresolved/foreign metadata uses a safe business default or one clarification, never fabricated ad membership or campaign eligibility. Shared knowledge plus selected Offer knowledge remains scoped.
- [ ] Preview mapping and replay inbound fixtures including a newly added ad, account-crossing ID, stale sync, unrelated source and returning lead. Verify durable context/version with current R09 authority at final reply.
- [ ] Use a permitted Meta test asset for real metadata verification before acceptance; no ad creation, spend changes or broadcast launch. This ticket manages inbound routing only.
